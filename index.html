Who flies you

The Sky is Our Canvas

Every flight departs from one of our private helipads across Bali and the Gili Islands. Every aircraft in our fleet was delivered factory-fresh and is maintained to the highest international aviation standards. Every pilot holds the top certifications in the field.

We are also proud to be Bali's first carbon-neutral helicopter operator. Each flight funds active reforestation across the archipelago. Because we believe that the highest luxury is one the earth can sustain.
